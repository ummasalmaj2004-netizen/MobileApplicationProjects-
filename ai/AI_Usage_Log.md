AI USAGE & CONTRIBUTION LOG
TDM Insight — Native Android Therapeutic Drug Monitoring Calculator

Purpose
This document records the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) as a supplementary resource throughout the planning, design, documentation, and refinement of the TDM Insight project. It highlights how AI-assisted suggestions were evaluated, adapted, and incorporated within the project development process.

1. AI Contribution Overview

AI assistance was utilized as a supportive resource to strengthen the project development process, particularly in:

Requirements Interpretation — highlighting important functional and non-functional requirements from the case study.
Interface Planning — exploring possible screen structures and user-flow arrangements.
System Design — supporting the organization of application components and workflow relationships.
Technical Documentation — identifying suitable structures for the Case Study Analysis and README documentation.
Project Licensing — exploring appropriate academic and non-commercial licensing considerations.
Design Refinement — providing alternative approaches that could be evaluated against the project's requirements.
Key Principle

AI suggestions served as guidance and reference points; the project team remained responsible for reviewing, modifying, verifying, and determining the final content and design decisions.

3. Representative AI Interaction
Example 1 — Interface & Documentation Planning

Purpose:
To identify and organize the project documentation and design materials required for development.

Representative interaction:

“Separate the wireframes and diagrams.”

Contribution highlighted:
AI provided guidance on treating the application's screen-level interface designs and system-level diagrams as separate design artifacts.

Team action:
The concepts were reviewed and the final wireframes and diagrams were organized separately according to the project documentation structure.

Example 2 — System Diagram Development

Purpose:
To explore an appropriate way to organize the application's system diagrams.

Representative interaction:

“Now the diagrams, can you group it into 3 and generate images?”

Contribution highlighted:
AI provided visual references demonstrating possible groupings and relationships between major application processes.

Team action:
The references were used to understand the proposed structure. The diagrams were subsequently manually recreated and refined in Draw.io.

Example 3 — Case Study Documentation

Purpose:
To organize the analysis of the TDM Insight case study.

Contribution highlighted:
AI suggested areas such as the problem statement, target users, functional requirements, workflows, validation, calculation engine, and proposed solution.

Team action:
The suggested structure was compared with the official case study and adjusted to represent the project's actual requirements.

4. Human Review & Adaptation

All AI-assisted suggestions were treated as proposals rather than final project decisions.

The team:

Reviewed the relevance of each suggestion.
Compared suggestions with the official project requirements.
Selected only applicable ideas.
Modified structures and wording where necessary.
Manually produced the final diagrams and design artifacts.
Ensured that the final documentation reflected the actual project scope and implementation.
5. Reflection

The use of AI provided an additional perspective during the development process by helping to organize ideas, identify important requirements, explore alternative design approaches, and improve documentation structure. Its contribution was particularly useful during the early planning and refinement stages of the project.

The team maintained responsibility for evaluating the suggestions and determining their suitability for the TDM Insight application. AI-assisted references were reviewed against the official case study and adapted where necessary. This approach allowed AI to function as a supporting development resource while preserving the team's own decision-making, design work, and project ownership.


AI was used to enhance the development process—not to replace the team's analysis, design decisions, or responsibility for the final project.
